EditorSetup Design Handbook

Visual source for the Registry: brief and design system.

Overview

This handbook is the design source for EditorSetup, a git-backed Registry of monospace Font and Theme Pairs. Each Pair carries Settings and a Snapshot for an Editor. Visitors browse the Registry, open a Pair, copy Settings, and Compare.

The shell stays zinc. Product color, type, and the mark live in the design system.
